NAME

data-engineer — moves raw data into trusted tables

SYNOPSIS

data-engineer [--stream | --batch] [--model dbt] [--scale PB] source... > warehouse

DESCRIPTION

[Your name] is a data engineer who designs, builds and operates the pipelines that carry data from operational systems to the people and models who depend on it. Sources are read once, transformed deterministically, and written idempotently, so that any run may be repeated without side effects.

  postgres ─┐
  kafka    ─┼─> extract ─> transform ─> validate ─> load ─> warehouse
  s3       ─┘    (cdc)      (spark)      (tests)    (delta)   (gold)

Operation is quiet by default. On failure, data-engineer fails loudly, early and upstream, before bad rows reach a dashboard.
